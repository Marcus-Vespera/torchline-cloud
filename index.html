Under the hood

What's running on
your box.

The stack behind the bundle — provisioned, patched and backed up by us. You see it in the desktop; you never have to operate it.

  • Vaultwarden. Team password manager on your own instance. Included on every size, not a licence line.
  • A database. PostgreSQL provisioned with your instance, or the connection details to plug your app into it.
  • Encrypted nightly backups. Offsite, versioned, restorable from the UI. Tested restores, not just "backups enabled".
  • TLS and DNS. Certificates issued and renewed for you, on your domain or a subdomain of ours.
  • Uptime and resource monitoring. Memory, disk and app health, visible in your desktop and watched by us.
  • Updates and patching. Base images, security patches and app upgrades handled on a maintenance window.

What you don't get: shell access, root, or a Docker console. That's deliberate. It's what keeps an instance reproducible and keeps "I broke it at 2am" from being your weekend.

What you do get: logs, environment variables, restart, app file browser, database browser and a one-click upgrade — all in the desktop UI.

Questions

The ones people actually ask.

How long does it take to get an instance?+

About fifteen minutes, unattended. You land in your desktop within seconds of paying and watch the build happen — server, base stack, database, then your apps. No ticket, no waiting for us to be at a keyboard.

What if I already have an app and a repo?+

That's a migration, and we do it as a fixed-price setup: we containerise your app, import your database and files, point your domain over with a rollback window, and hand you the instance. It needs a Dockerfile-able app, data under about 50 GB and a database we already run. If it doesn't fit those, we'll tell you before you pay.

Do I have to use Torchflow?+

No — but it's included, so there's no reason not to. Use it to build something new without a developer, or ignore it and deploy a catalog app or your own repo instead. It's a tool that comes with the instance, not a requirement for using it.

Can it run a Telegram bot or a webhook that has to stay online?+

Yes. Bots and always-on workers run on your instance, which is monitored and restarted if it falls over. That's usually the thing people can't do from a laptop or a free tier — the process dies when the laptop closes.

Do I get SSH?+

No — and that's the product. You get logs, environment variables, restart, a file browser, a database browser and deployments from the UI. Everything you need to run the app, nothing that lets you take the box down for everyone else on it.

What happens if my app outgrows the instance?+

You upgrade in the UI and we resize it during a short maintenance window. Your data, domains and configuration move with it. Growth is a bigger box, not a new line item.

Can I run adult content?+

Not on the shared cloud. Legal adult brands are hosted on a separate provider, with their own IP, separate payment rails and an age gate — priced +$50/mo. Ask before you deploy, not after.

Is there a contract or SLA?+

Month to month, cancel any time. No SLA: we promise monitored, backed up and best effort, in writing, and we'd rather say that plainly than publish a 99.9% we can't back with a credit.
